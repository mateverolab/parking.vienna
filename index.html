Other signs you might run into
Craftsperson sticker (Handwerkerparkkleber) Registered trade vehicles may park beyond the 2-hour limit, Mon–Fri 8:00–16:00, in the districts where it's approved.
Event closures Rathausplatz, Stadthalle, Ernst-Happel-Stadion and similar sites post temporary tow-away zones for markets, concerts or matches — dates are always signed on-site in advance.
EV charging bays Marked for charging only — parking there without actually charging can get you towed.
Weekly markets Streets hosting markets (Naschmarkt, Brunnenmarkt, etc.) ban parking during market hours, several days a week.
Taxi stands & street cleaning "Taxi" bays are reserved at all times; occasional zip-tied signs suspend parking temporarily for street cleaning — check the pole, not just the main sign.

General rule (all 23 districts, since 2022)

Mon–Fri, 9:00–22:00: paid zone city-wide. Parkschein required, max. 2 hours without a Parkpickerl.

Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays: free parking in most of the city (except special zones like Stadthalle).

2026 rate: €1.70 per 30 min. Free for the first 15 min, but you still must fill out the Parkschein.

With a Parkpickerl for your district: no time limit, no cost — except on business streets.

How to pay

Besides the paper Parkschein, you can pay by phone with HANDYPARKEN, the official app: register once, top up credit, and start/stop your parking session from your phone — no need to display anything on the dashboard.
